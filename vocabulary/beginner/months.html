Months of the Year

Introduction

There are twelve months in a year. We use them for birthdays, holidays and dates.

In this lesson, you will:

  • Learn the months of the year.

Warm-up

Read the question. Say your answer.

Question: When is your birthday?

Example answer: My birthday is in May.

There are 12 months in a year.

in + month

  • My birthday is in June.
  • It is cold in January.
  • I go on holiday in August.

Be careful!

Months start with a big letter: June ✔️   june ❌

My birthday is in March.

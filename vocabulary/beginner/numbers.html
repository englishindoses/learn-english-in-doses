Numbers 0 to 100

Introduction

Numbers are very important. We use numbers every day.

In this lesson, you will:

  • Learn the numbers 0 to 100.

Warm-up

Read the question. Say your answer.

Question: How many brothers and sisters do you have?

Example answer: I have two brothers and one sister.

21 to 99

twenty + one = twenty-one

  • 21 = twenty-one
  • 34 = thirty-four
  • 58 = fifty-eight
  • 99 = ninety-nine

Be careful!

13 = thirteen   30 = thirty

14 = fourteen   40 = forty (not fourty)

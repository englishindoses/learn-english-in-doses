Days of the Week

Introduction

There are seven days in a week. We use them every day.

In this lesson, you will:

  • Learn the days of the week.

Warm-up

Read the question. Say your answer.

Question: What day is it today?

Example answer: It is Monday.

The Days of the Week

DayShort
MondayMon
TuesdayTue
WednesdayWed
ThursdayThu
FridayFri
SaturdaySat
SundaySun

Saturday and Sunday = the weekend

on + day

  • I work on Monday.
  • I go to the gym on Tuesday.
  • See you on Friday!

Be careful!

Days start with a big letter: Monday ✔️   monday ❌

Wednesday: we say Wenz-day.

Reading

Read the text.

Hi! My name is Leo.

I work on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday.

On Saturday, I play football.

On Sunday, I have lunch with my family.

My favourite day is Friday!

Seasons

Introduction

There are four seasons in a year. Each season has different weather.

In this lesson, you will:

  • Learn the four seasons.

Warm-up

Read the question. Say your answer.

Question: What's your favourite season?

Example answer: My favourite season is summer.

The Four Seasons

SeasonPictureWeatherMonths (in the UK)
spring🌸warmMarch, April, May
summer☀️hotJune, July, August
autumn🍂coolSeptember, October, November
winter❄️coldDecember, January, February

in + season

  • It is hot in summer.
  • It is cold in winter.
  • I go to the park in spring.

Good to know!

In American English, autumn = fall.

In Australia, December is in summer!

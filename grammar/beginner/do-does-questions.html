Questions with Do and Does

Questions you can use today — in a shop, on the street, meeting someone new

This worksheet practises questions with do and does, with and without a question word (what, where, when, why, who, how). You will build the questions, choose the right one for a situation, and answer when someone asks you.

Quick Grammar Review

1. Yes / No questions

Form: Do / Does + subject + verb (base form)?

  1. Do you have a menu?
  2. Does your teacher speak Spanish?
  3. Do the shops open on Sunday?

Use do with I, you, we, they. Use does with he, she, it.

Short answers: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  |  Yes, it does. / No, it doesn't.

2. Wh- questions

Form: Wh- word + do / does + subject + verb (base form)?

  1. Where do I sign?
  2. Who do you work with?
  3. What time does the film start?

These questions need a real answer, not yes or no: "At the bottom." / "My friend Ana." / "At eight o'clock."

Activity 3: Choose the Best Answer

Read each situation and choose the best question or the best answer.

1. You want to cook fish for your friend Ana. First you ask her:

A. Do you eating fish?
B. Do you eat fish?
C. Does you eat fish?

2. Your printer doesn't work. You don't know who fixes it.

A. What do I call about the printer?
B. When do I call about the printer?
C. Who do I call about the printer?

3. Someone at work asks: "Do you drive?" You don't drive.

A. No, I'm not.
B. No, I doesn't.
C. No, I don't.

4. A friend asks: "Does your brother live in London?" He lives in Leeds.

A. No, he doesn't. He lives in Leeds.
B. No, he don't. He lives in Leeds.
C. No, he isn't. He lives in Leeds.

5. You want to know: does your friend come to work by bus, by bike or by car?

A. How do you get to work?
B. When do you get to work?
C. Why do you get to work?

6. Your friend goes to bed at nine o'clock every night. You want to know the reason.

A. When do you go to bed so early?
B. Why do you go to bed so early?
C. Where do you go to bed so early?

7. A friend asks: "Where does your sister work?"

A. Every day.
B. In a hospital.
C. Yes, she does.

8. You want to know how many times a week the cleaner comes.

A. How long does the cleaner come?
B. How much does the cleaner come?
C. How often does the cleaner come?

Activity 4: Write the Missing Word

It's Maria's first week at work. She and Tom are talking in the kitchen. Write one word in each gap.

Maria: No, I . It takes me about forty minutes.

Maria: do you do here?

Tom: I'm a designer.

Maria: does everyone go for lunch?

Tom: There's a small café near the office. We all go there.

Tom: you play any sport?

Maria: Yes, I . I go to the gym most days.

Tom: Which gym you go to?

Maria: The one on Mill Street. anyone from work go there?

Tom: Kasia goes sometimes, but she go in the mornings. She gets up late.

Tom: you want to grab a coffee after work?

Maria: Yes, good idea!

Can / Can't

Introduction

Can is a very useful verb. Use it to talk about what you are able to do, to ask if something is OK, and to ask people for help.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Talk about what you can and can't do.
  • Ask for permission with Can I...?
  • Ask for help with Can you...?

1. Ability

Form: subject + can / can't + base verb

When to use it: Use it to say what you are able to do.

  1. I can swim.
  2. She can speak three languages.
  3. My dad can't cook.

Can is the same for every subject, and the next verb has no to: she can swim (NOT she cans swim, NOT she can to swim). Questions: Can you drive? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

2. Permission

Form: Can I + base verb...?

When to use it: Use it to ask if it's OK to do something.

  1. Can I sit here?
  2. Can I use your phone?
  3. Can I leave early today?

You can't + verb means it's not allowed: You can't park here.

3. Requests

Form: Can you + base verb...?

When to use it: Use it to ask someone to do something for you.

  1. Can you help me?
  2. Can you open the window, please?
  3. Can you call me back later?

Summary: Can / Can't

Use Form Example
Ability (+) can + verb I can swim.
Ability (−) can't + verb He can't cook.
Ability (?) Can + subject + verb? Can you drive? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
Permission Can I + verb? Can I sit here?
Request Can you + verb? Can you help me?

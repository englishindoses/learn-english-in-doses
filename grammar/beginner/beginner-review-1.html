Review: Be and Present Simple

Introduction

This review brings together section 1: the verb be, the present simple, prepositions of time and place, adverbs of frequency, and like, love and hate.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Use be and the present simple to talk about yourself and other people.
  • Use in, on, at and adverbs of frequency to talk about your routine.
  • Say what you like and don't like.

Quick Reminder

Lesson Remember Example
The verb be I am, he / she / it is, you / we / they are She's a nurse. Are you tired?
Present simple he / she / it + -s; don't / doesn't; do / does? He works from home. Does she drive?
Prepositions at + time, on + day, in + month / year I start work at nine on Mondays.
Adverbs of frequency before the main verb, after be I usually walk. I'm always late.
Like, love, hate + noun or verb-ing I love cooking. I don't like it.

Final Review

Introduction

Well done for getting to the end of the beginner course! This final review mixes grammar from all five sections, so you can see what you remember and what to study again.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Check what you remember from the whole beginner course.
  • Talk about the present, the past and the future.
  • Find the lessons you need to study again.

Quick Reminder

Section Grammar Example
1. Be and Present Simple am / is / are; he works; don't / doesn't; in / on / at; always / never; love + -ing She's a nurse. He usually works from home.
2. Nouns, Possessives, Imperatives and Can this / these; my / your; Tom's; there is / there are; imperatives; can / can't There's a café next to my office.
3. Past Simple and Present Continuous was / were; worked / went; didn't / did; am / is / are + -ing I went to Rome last year. She's working now.
4. Quantifiers, Comparatives and Superlatives much / many / a lot of; some / any; would like; -er / more; the -est / the most I'd like some water. It's the best café in town.
5. Future Forms and Modal Verbs going to; will; should; have to I'm going to visit my mum. You should rest.

Review: Quantifiers, Comparatives and Superlatives

Introduction

This review brings together section 4: countable and uncountable nouns, much, many and a lot of, some and any, would like, and comparing things.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Use much, many, a lot of, some and any.
  • Ask for things and make offers with would like.
  • Compare things with comparatives and superlatives.

Quick Reminder

Lesson Remember Example
Countable and uncountable many + plural; much + uncountable; a lot of + both How many people? How much time?
Some and any some: positive, offers, requests; any: negatives, questions We need some milk. Do you have any eggs?
Would like I'd like...; Would you like...? I'd like a coffee. Would you like a drink?
Comparatives -er / more + than cheaper than, more expensive than
Superlatives the -est / the most the cheapest, the most expensive

The Past Simple (Week 1)

Introduction

We use the past simple to talk about finished actions in the past — yesterday, last week, in 2019.

By the end of this lesson you can:

  • Use regular and irregular past forms correctly.
  • Make negatives and questions with "did" + base verb.
  • Ask and answer past simple questions about your own life.

2. The forms

Positive

subjectpast form…
I / He / She / We / Theywatcheda film last night.
My sisterwentto the market on Saturday.

The past form is the same for every subject. No "-s", ever.

Negative

subjectdidn'tbase verb…
I / She / Theydidn'tworkon Friday.
The liftdidn'tstopon my floor.

Questions

(wh- word)didsubjectbase verb…
—Didyoupaythe bill?
Wheredidsheparkthe car?
What timedidthe shopclose?

Short answers: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

The key point: after "did" and "didn't", the verb is always the base form. The word "did" already carries the past.
Where did you went? → Where did you go?
I didn't bought it. → I didn't buy it.

Regular or irregular?

TypeRuleExamples
Regular+ "-ed"work → worked, cook → cooked
consonant + "-y" → "-ied"study → studied
short vowel → double letterstop → stopped
Irregulara new word — learn each onego → went, buy → bought, take → took

Good news: you only need the past form in positive sentences. In negatives and questions the verb goes back to the base form.

"Was" / "were" — different

subjectbe…
I / he / she / itwasat home.
you / we / theywerelate.

Negative: wasn't / weren't.   Question: Was he tired? Were you busy?

The verb "be" never uses "did". Did you be tired?

8. Speaking: answer the question

Choose a topic, then click the button. Answer in two or three sentences — not one word.

#

While you speak

  • Say when: yesterday, last night, two years ago.
  • Add a reason: … because I was tired.
  • Ask back: And you? What did you do?
  • Careful: after "did", use the base verb.

Example

What did you do yesterday evening?

I finished work at six and I went to the supermarket. I didn't cook — I was too tired, so I ordered a pizza. And you?

9. Speaking: tell the story

Task: a day that went wrong

Think of a day when things did not go to plan — a bad journey, a difficult day at work, a holiday problem. Tell your teacher the story. Speak for about two minutes.

Use these questions to build the story:

  • When was it? Where were you?
  • What happened first?
  • What went wrong?
  • What did you do?
  • How did it finish?
Challenge — include all four:
  • three irregular verbs
  • one negative sentence with "didn't"
  • one sentence with "was" or "were"
  • two questions for your teacher at the end

Your teacher will listen, then ask you questions about the story. Answer in full sentences.

Remember

  1. Positive: use the past form — I went, she bought, we worked.
  2. Negative and question: "did" / "didn't" + base verb — I didn't go. Did she buy it?
  3. was / were never use "did".

Homework: repeat activities 1, 4 and 6 before the next lesson.

Past Simple: Be

Introduction

Was and were are the past of be. Use them to say where people were and how things were. They're the easiest past forms, and you'll use them a lot.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Use was and were to talk about the past.
  • Make negatives with wasn't and weren't.
  • Ask questions with was and were.

1. Positive Sentences

Form: I / he / she / it + was / you / we / they + were

When to use it: Use it to talk about places, feelings and things in the past.

  1. I was at work all day.
  2. The film was great.
  3. We were in Spain last week.

2. Negative Sentences

Form: wasn't (was not) / weren't (were not)

When to use it: Add not after was or were.

  1. I wasn't at home last night.
  2. The hotel wasn't very clean.
  3. They weren't at the meeting.

3. Questions

Form: Was / Were + subject + ...?

When to use it: Put was or were before the subject.

  1. Was the party good?
  2. Were you at work yesterday?
  3. How was your weekend?

Be never uses did. Say Were you tired? (NOT Did you be tired?). Short answers: Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. / Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.

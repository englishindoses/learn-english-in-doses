Superlative Adjectives

Introduction

Use superlative adjectives to say that something is number one in a group: the cheapest, the most beautiful, the best. Always use the before them.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Make superlatives with the + -est.
  • Use the most with long adjectives.
  • Use the best, the worst and the furthest.

1. Short Adjectives: The -est

Form: the + adjective + -est

When to use it: Use it with short adjectives to say something is number one.

  1. He's the oldest person in our office.
  2. This is the cheapest hotel in town.
  3. August is the hottest month.

Spelling is the same as for comparatives: big → the biggest, happy → the happiest, nice → the nicest.

2. Long Adjectives: The Most

Form: the most + adjective

When to use it: Use the most with longer adjectives.

  1. It's the most beautiful beach in Portugal.
  2. This is the most popular café in the area.
  3. She's the most organised person I know.

3. Irregular Superlatives

Form: good → the best, bad → the worst, far → the furthest

When to use it: These don't follow the rules. Learn them one by one.

  1. My mum makes the best pasta.
  2. That was the worst film of the year.
  3. Who lives the furthest from the office?

Summary: Superlative Adjectives

Adjective Comparative Superlative
old older the oldest
big bigger the biggest
happy happier the happiest
expensive more expensive the most expensive
good better the best
bad worse the worst
far further the furthest

Test Yourself!

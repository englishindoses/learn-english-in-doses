Review: Nouns, Possessives, Imperatives and Can

Introduction

This review brings together section 2: this and that, plural nouns, possessives, there is and there are, imperatives, and can.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Use this, that, these and those with singular and plural nouns.
  • Say who things belong to with my, your, his, her and 's.
  • Describe places, give instructions and ask for help.

Quick Reminder

Lesson Remember Example
This, that + possessive adjectives this = near, that = far; my, your, his, her, its, our, their This is my phone. Is that your car?
These, those + plural nouns -s, -es, -ies; children, people; these / those These are my children. Are those your keys?
Possessive 's person + 's; plural + ' Anna's husband. My parents' house.
There is / there are there's + singular; there are + plural There's a café opposite my office.
Imperatives base verb; don't + verb Turn left. Don't forget your keys.
Can / can't can + base verb I can swim. Can you help me?

Test Yourself!

Choose the correct answer for each question.

1. (You're holding them.) _____ shoes are really nice.

A. This
B. These
C. Those

2. Hi, I'm Sam. What's _____ name?

A. you
B. your
C. yours

3. Peter lives with _____ girlfriend.

A. her
B. his
C. their

4. My _____ names are Luke and Emma.

A. children
B. children's
C. childrens'

5. _____ two supermarkets near my house.

A. There is
B. There are
C. It is

6. The bank is _____ the post office and the café.

A. between
B. under
C. behind

7. _____ your phone in the cinema.

A. Don't use
B. No use
C. Not use

8. I _____ swim. I never learned.

B. can't
C. don't

9. _____ I use your pen?

A. Can
B. Do
C. Am

A. man
B. men
C. mans

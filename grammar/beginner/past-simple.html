Past Simple: Regular and Irregular Verbs

Introduction

Use the past simple to talk about finished actions in the past: what you did yesterday, last week or last year. Regular verbs add -ed. Irregular verbs have their own past form.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Make the past simple of regular verbs with -ed.
  • Use common irregular past forms, like went, had and saw.
  • Say when something happened with yesterday, last and ago.

1. Regular Verbs

Form: verb + -ed (the same for every subject)

When to use it: Use it for finished actions in the past.

  1. I worked late yesterday.
  2. We watched a film last night.
  3. She called her mum on Sunday.

Spelling: live → lived, study → studied, stop → stopped.

2. Irregular Verbs

Form: a special past form for each verb

When to use it: Many common verbs are irregular. Learn them one by one.

  1. I went to the gym after work.
  2. We had pizza for dinner.
  3. He got up at six.

Common irregular verbs: go → went, have → had, get → got, see → saw, make → made, take → took, buy → bought, eat → ate, come → came, do → did, leave → left, meet → met, catch → caught, say → said, give → gave.

3. Past Time Words

Form: past simple + yesterday / last + week, month, year / ... ago

When to use it: Use these words to say when something happened.

  1. I saw Tom yesterday.
  2. We moved here two years ago.
  3. They visited us last weekend.

Summary: Past Simple: Regular and Irregular Verbs

Type Verb Past simple Example
Regular work worked I worked late.
Regular live lived We lived in Rome.
Regular study studied She studied English.
Regular stop stopped The rain stopped.
Irregular go went We went home.
Irregular have had I had a shower.
Irregular see saw They saw a film.
Irregular buy bought He bought a car.

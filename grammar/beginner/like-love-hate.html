Like, Love and Hate + -ing

Introduction

Use love, like and hate to talk about the things you enjoy and the things you don't. After these verbs you can use a noun or a verb + -ing. Object pronouns like it and them help you not repeat words.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Say what you love, like, don't like and hate.
  • Use a verb + -ing after love, like and hate.
  • Use object pronouns (me, you, him, her, it, us, them) after a verb.

1. Love, Like, Hate + Noun

Form: subject + love / like / don't like / hate + noun

When to use it: Use these verbs to say how you feel about things.

  1. I love pizza.
  2. She doesn't like cold weather.
  3. My son hates vegetables.

He / she / it: loves, likes, doesn't like, hates.

2. Love, Like, Hate + Verb-ing

Form: subject + love / like / hate + verb-ing

When to use it: Use verb-ing to talk about activities.

  1. I love cooking.
  2. He likes playing football.
  3. We hate getting up early.

Spelling: cook → cooking, dance → dancing, swim → swimming.

3. Object Pronouns

Form: verb + me / you / him / her / it / us / them

When to use it: Use an object pronoun after a verb, so you don't repeat the name or the thing.

  1. Do you like tea? — Yes, I love it.
  2. My boss is really nice. Everyone likes him.
  3. I don't like spiders. I hate them.

Summary: Like, Love and Hate + -ing

+ noun + verb-ing
love I love music. I love dancing.

don't like We don't like rain. We don't like walking in the rain.

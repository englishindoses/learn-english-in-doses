Review: Past Simple and Present Continuous

Introduction

This review brings together section 3: was and were, the past simple, past negatives and questions, the present continuous, and when to use the present simple or the present continuous.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Talk about the past with was, were and the past simple.
  • Talk about now with the present continuous.
  • Choose between the present simple and the present continuous.

Quick Reminder

Lesson Remember Example
Past simple: be was / were; wasn't / weren't I was tired. Were you at work?
Past simple: positive verb + -ed, or the irregular form I worked late. We went home.
Past simple: negatives and questions didn't / did + base verb I didn't go. Did you go?
Present continuous am / is / are + verb-ing She's working from home today.
Present simple vs continuous routines and facts vs now I usually walk, but today I'm driving.

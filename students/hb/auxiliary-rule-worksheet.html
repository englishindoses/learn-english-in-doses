2. The rule

auxiliary + subject + base verb

After do, does, did, don't, doesn't, didn't, can, can't, must, mustn't, will, won't the next verb never changes. It is always the base form.

  • ✗ Does the bakery opens at six?  →  ✓ Does the bakery open at six?  (no -s)
  • ✗ They didn't finished the roof.  →  ✓ They didn't finish the roof.  (no past form)
  • ✗ You must to see this square.  →  ✓ You must see this square.  (no "to")

One verb carries the grammar — the other one stays simple.

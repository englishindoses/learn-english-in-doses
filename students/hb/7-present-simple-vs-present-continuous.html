Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Introduction

We use the present simple for facts and habits. We use the present continuous for things happening now or for a short time.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Say where people work and what they usually do.
  • Describe what people are doing in a picture.
  • Talk about your usual routine and what is different this week.

Warm-up: At the Bakery

  1. Where do these people work? What do you think they usually do there?
  2. What is each person doing right now?

Sort the Sentences

Choose Fact or habit for something that is generally true or happens regularly. Choose Now or temporary for something happening now or for a short time.

Mia teaches maths.
Leo is fixing a bike right now.
Mia is helping a student at the moment.
Leo works in a bike shop.
We buy our bread here every Saturday.
This week, I’m staying with my sister.
Nina lives near the station.
The baker is taking bread out of the oven.

Present Simple

Form: subject + base verb. With he, she and it, usually add -s. Use don’t / doesn’t + base verb for negatives and do / does + subject + base verb for questions. With be, use am / is / are.

When to use it: Use it for facts and things you do regularly.

  1. She is a baker.
    (= That’s her job.)
  2. He starts work at six every morning.
    (= That’s his routine.)
  3. What time do you finish work on Fridays?
    (= I’m asking about your usual Friday.)

Present Continuous

Form: subject + am / is / are + verb-ing. Add not after am / is / are for negatives. Put am / is / are before the subject in questions.

When to use it: Use it for actions happening now or for a short time around now.

  1. She is making bread.
    (= She’s doing it now.)
  2. I’m not driving to work this week.
    (= This week is different from usual.)
  3. What are you working on today?
    (= I’m asking about your work today.)

Practice: Sam’s Lunch Break

Choose the correct form of each verb to complete the story.

Sam a doctor. He usually at the hospital near his home. This week he at a clinic in town. It’s lunchtime and he a sandwich right now. He usually lunch at his desk. Today he outside in the sun.

Speaking: Your Daily Life

Answer both parts of each question. Talk about what you usually do, then say what is happening around now.

  1. What do you usually do before work, and what are you doing differently this week?
  2. How do you usually get around your city, and how are you getting around this week?
  3. What do you usually do at work, and what are you working on at the moment?
  4. What do you usually eat at lunchtime, and what are you eating more often these days?
  5. Where do you usually go when you need a break, and what are you doing to relax these days?
  6. What do you usually watch or listen to after work, and what are you watching or listening to these days?
  7. How do you usually practise English, and what are you doing to improve it this month?
  8. What do you usually spend money on, and what are you saving for at the moment?
  9. Who do you usually see at weekends, and who are you spending more time with these days?

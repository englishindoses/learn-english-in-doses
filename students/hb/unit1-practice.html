Unit 1 Practice

Let's practise!

Today, we will practise questions, past events and words like can, must and should. Start with short choices, then build sentences and talk about your life.

After this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Ask questions and make negatives with do, does and did.
  • Talk about past events.
  • Use can, must and should with the base verb.

Activity 1: The auxiliary rule

Choose the correct answer.

1. Does your sister ____ near you?

A. live
B. lives
C. to live

2. He doesn't ____ coffee.

A. drinks
B. drink
C. to drink

3. ____ your parents work on Saturdays?

A. Does
B. Has
C. Do

4. Does your father ____ to work?

A. drives
B. to drive
C. drive

5. Did you ____ the message?

A. get
B. got
C. to get

6. We didn't ____ a taxi.

A. took
B. take
C. to take

7. She can ____ very well.

A. swims
B. to swim
C. swim

8. You must ____ here.

A. stop
B. stops
C. to stop

Speaking: Your life

Answer in full sentences. Then ask your teacher the same questions.

  1. What do you do after work? What don't you do?
  2. What did you do yesterday? What didn't you do?
  3. Where were you last Sunday?
  4. What can you do well? What can't you do?
  5. What must people do at your workplace? What mustn't they do?
  6. I'm very tired. What should I do? What shouldn't I do?

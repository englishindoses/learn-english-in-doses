Block A Revision

How much do you remember?

Check your verb forms in short sentences about daily life and work. Start with the warm-up, then complete the four activities.

After this revision, you will be able to:

  • Use do and does in present simple questions and negatives.
  • Make past simple positive sentences, negatives and questions.
  • Use can/can't, must/mustn't and should/shouldn't with the correct verb form.

Warm-up: Right or wrong?

Is the grammar right or wrong? Choose one answer for each sentence.

1. Does your father drive to work? /
2. I didn't went to the office yesterday. /
3. She can to swim. /
You got 0 out of 3.

Activity 3: Modal verbs

Choose the grammatically correct words. There is only one correct answer.

1. I can ___.

A. swims
B. swim
C. to swim

2. She ___ a car.

A. can't drive
B. can't to drive
C. doesn't can drive

3. On this building site, everyone ___ a hard hat.

A. must wears
B. must to wear
C. must wear

4. You ___ in this office. Smoking is forbidden.

A. mustn't smoke
B. mustn't to smoke
C. don't must smoke

5. You look tired. You ___ a break.

A. should to take
B. should take
C. should takes

6. He ___ his phone while driving.

A. shouldn't uses
B. doesn't should use
C. shouldn't use

7. ___ me with this suitcase?

A. Do you can help
B. Can you help
C. Can you to help

8. Where ___ the client tomorrow?

A. should we meet
B. we should meet
C. should we to meet

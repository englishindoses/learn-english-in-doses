# Hebert — Remedial Grammar Syllabus 2026
Built from **Errors from Diagnostic Quiz 1** + spoken errors in `Mistakes.odt`.
1 hour/week **+ interactive practice as homework**, so every lesson = short focus + recycling + speaking,
with retention work pushed out of the lesson.

### From spoken errors (`Mistakes.odt`)
- *I don't decide* → **I haven't decided** (present perfect for unfinished/recent)
- **yet vs still** (and *already*)
- ***can to*** — same fault line as the modal error above
- **has vs there is/are** (*In my city has a park* — classic Portuguese *tem*)
- **music / song** (countable vs uncountable + false friends)
- ***good to the company*** → **good for** (dependent prepositions/collocation)

### The pattern under the pattern
Three transfer errors from Portuguese cause most of the damage:
1. **Auxiliary system** — he doesn't strip the main verb after *do/did/can/must* (*didn't bought*, *can to*, *did you went*, *Has your father drive*).
2. ***tem*** → *has* instead of *there is/are*.
3. **Preposition mapping** — Portuguese preposition translated word-for-word.
Fix these three and intelligibility jumps more than any other work.

## 2. The plan — 13 weeks (~3 months)

10 teaching weeks + 3 revision/assessment weeks. Each lesson: 10 min recycle of earlier weeks →
15 min focus → 30 min speaking task that forces the target → 5 min error feedback.
Every teaching week ends with an interactive practice set as homework.

### Block A — The verb engine ← highest priority
| Wk | Focus | Kills |
|---|---|---|

| **1** ✅ | **Past simple** — *did* + base verb, irregular forms, negatives, question word order. **DONE** (interactive lesson `week1-past-simple.html`) | *didn't bought*, *Where did you went* |

| 3 | **REVISION + PROGRESS CHECK 1** — auxiliary system across present, past & modals. No new grammar. | |

## 3. Deliberate decisions

- **Present perfect vs past simple is NOT in this round.** *I don't decide* → *I haven't decided*,
  plus *yet / still / already / just*. It's the hardest item for a BR Portuguese speaker and only
  lands once the past simple and the auxiliary rule are automatic. **→ First topic of the next
  block (week 14).** Note it in `Mistakes.odt` when it comes up, correct it in passing, don't teach it yet.
- **Prepositions of movement** (*meet you to the bus stop*) rides along in week 10's recycling
  slot rather than taking a lesson — one error, not a system.
- **Order is by damage done, not by coursebook order.** Block A is what makes him hard to
  understand; everything after it is polish by comparison.
- **13 weeks assumes homework actually gets done.** The profile says he doesn't usually do
  homework — if the interactive practice isn't happening, this stretches back towards 18 weeks
  and the revision weeks (3, 7, 13) become the only retention mechanism he has.

## 4. Rules for every lesson
- Never teach the grammar cold — elicit it from something he said.
- Because he misunderstands without showing it: end each stage by asking him to **explain the
  rule back in his own words** or apply it to his own life.
- Log every spoken error to `Mistakes.odt`; the first 10 minutes of the next lesson comes from it.
- If a Block A item resurfaces in week 11, it goes back in the recycle slot. Block A items are
  never "done" — including week 0.
- Set the interactive practice at the end of every teaching week and check it at the start of
  the next lesson. It is the only spaced repetition he gets.

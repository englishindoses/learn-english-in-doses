Simple Past Tense - Review

We use the past simple to talk about finished actions in the past — yesterday, last week, in 2019.

By the end of this lesson you can:

  1. Use regular and irregular past forms correctly.
  2. Make negatives and questions with did + base verb.
  3. Use was and were correctly.

We take one form at a time: learn it, practise it, then move on. At the end you put everything together.

Negative Sentences

subjectdidn'tbase verb…
I / She / Theydidn'tworkon Friday.
The liftdidn'tstopon my floor.

Good news: the past form you just practised is only for positive sentences. Here the verb goes back to the base form.

The key point: after didn't, the verb is always the base form. The word didn't already carries the past.
I didn't bought it. → I didn't buy it.

#

While you speak

  • Say when: yesterday, last night, two years ago.
  • Add a reason: … because I was tired.
  • Ask back: And you? What did you do?
  • Careful: after did, use the base verb.

Example

What did you do yesterday evening?

I finished work at six and I went to the supermarket. I didn't cook — I was too tired, so I ordered a pizza. And you?

Remember

  1. Positive: use the past form — I went, she bought, we worked.
  2. Negative: didn't + base verb — I didn't go. She didn't buy it.
  3. Question: did + subject + base verb — Did she buy it? Where did you go?
  4. was / were never use did.

👋 Lesson 1: Meeting Your Group

What will you learn?

In this lesson, you will learn how to:

  • Greet your group and introduce yourself
  • Ask people their name and where they are from
  • Say what you like and don't like

📖 Meeting the Group

Read this conversation. Paulo is a tour guide. Anna is a tourist.

  1. Paulo: Good morning! Welcome to Cachoeiras.
  2. Paulo: My name is Paulo. I'm your guide today.
  3. Anna: Nice to meet you.
  4. Paulo: What's your name?
  5. Anna: I'm Anna.
  6. Paulo: Where are you from?
  7. Anna: I'm from Germany.

❤️ Saying What You Like

Use these to talk to your group and to learn about them.

  1. I like birds.
  2. I don't like snakes.
  3. Do you like waterfalls?
  4. Yes, I do.
  5. No, I don't.
